Server rules

The Laws of Olympus

Few, clear, and equal for everyone. By joining the server you accept them — and not knowing them is no excuse. Set in stone: /rules

1 · Community & chat

1.1Respect always: no insults, harassment, hate or toxicity towards other players or the staff.
1.2No spam, flood or all-caps chains in global chat.
1.3Advertising other servers or communities is forbidden.
1.4No personal data — neither yours nor anyone else's.

2 · Fair play

2.1Hacks, cheats, X-ray, macros and any mod that gives an unfair advantage are forbidden.
2.2No exploits or item duplication. If you find a bug, report it in Support — abusing it means a punishment.
2.3Don't combat-log: disconnecting in the middle of combat carries an automatic penalty.

3 · Building & claims

3.1Griefing is forbidden: don't break, steal from or modify other players' builds, protected or not.
3.2Protect your base with claims — it's your responsibility. Learn how.
3.3No offensive builds or lag machines (farms that tank the TPS).

4 · Economy & trading

4.1Scams in player-to-player deals are forbidden and get reversed.
4.2Selling items, in-game money or accounts for real money is forbidden.
4.3Don't abuse pricing or market errors — report them.

5 · PvP & events

5.1PvP outside events is consensual: if the other player doesn't want to fight, respect that.
5.2During events, the event rules apply — announced before the start.
5.3Killing by exploiting lag, spawn traps or safe zones is punishable.

6 · Accounts

6.1One main account per person. Using alts to evade punishments means a permanent ban.
6.2Your account is your responsibility: whatever it does counts as yours.
6.3Offensive nicknames and skins are not allowed.

Punishment ladder

Warning Mute Kick Temp ban Permanent ban

Staff applies punishments based on severity and history — serious cases (hacks, duping, mass grief) go straight to a ban. Think a punishment is unfair? Appeal in Support.
